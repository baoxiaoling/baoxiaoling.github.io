# Privacy Policy for Folio

**Last updated:** November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Folio** ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile and desktop application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We strictly respect your privacy. **Folio is a local-first application and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal, private, or sensitive user data.**

* **Notes and Vault Data:** All notes, Markdown documents, drawings, attachments, version history, and settings you create or open in Folio remain locally on your device. We have no servers and cannot access your files or content.
* **Personal Information:** We do not require user accounts or sign-ins, and we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.
* **Device and Usage Data:** We do not track device identifiers, location data, IP addresses, telemetry, or app usage metrics.
* **Financial Data:** We do not process, collect, or store any payment or financial information.

## 2. Permissions and File Access

The App operates locally on your device and requests access only to resources you explicitly choose to use:

* **Files and Folders:** Folio reads and writes only within the App's private vault storage or specific folders and files you explicitly select using your operating system's standard file and folder pickers (such as Android's Storage Access Framework or macOS Finder).
* **Device Permissions:** On Android, the App does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions. On macOS, microphone and on-device speech recognition permissions are requested only if you choose to record an audio clip into a note or index local media for search; all recordings and transcripts stay locally on your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or external tracking SDKs that gather or process personal data. All bundled open-source libraries (for editing, Markdown, math, diagrams, drawings, and PDF viewing) run locally offline within the App.

If you choose to place a vault in a folder synced by a third-party cloud service on your device (such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or Box), enable operating-system device backups, open external links or embedded web media, or connect optional local command-line tools on desktop, any data handled by those external services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
