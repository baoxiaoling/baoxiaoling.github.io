# Privacy Policy for Battleship

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Battleship** ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We strictly respect your privacy. **Battleship does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal, private, or sensitive user data.**

* **Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.
* **Device and Usage Data:** We do not track device identifiers, location data, IP addresses, or app usage metrics.
* **Financial Data:** We do not process, collect, or store any payment or financial information.

## 2. Permissions

The App operates entirely locally on your device and does not require or request access to sensitive device permissions (such as contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location).

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or external tracking SDKs that gather or process personal data.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
